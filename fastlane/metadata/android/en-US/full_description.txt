Work out what you don't know from what you do. Rule of 3 solves everyday calculations in seconds: how much do 5 kilos cost if 3 cost $2,400, how many days do 6 people need for a job 4 people finish in 12, what a 25 % discount or the VAT comes to.

You don't need to know math: type what you know, see the answer instantly and, if you like, read how it was worked out in plain words.

• Direct (more and more), inverse (more and less) and compound (several things at once) rule of three
• Percentages, increases, discounts and 19 % VAT
• Step-by-step explanations in everyday language
• Exact results, no rounding errors
• Examples to try and help to choose what to use
• Light and dark mode
• Works offline, no accounts, no ads: your calculations stay on your phone
